01 About

Most of my favorite problems sit where language models meet messy real data, like clinical notes, security logs, freight records or satellite pixels.

I'm a Data Science grad from the University of North Texas, with a CS undergrad (data science specialization, math minor) from Shiv Nadar. My playground spans multi-agent architectures, RAG pipelines and full-stack platforms.

I care about things that just work: clean APIs, honest evals, efficient models, and interfaces people actually want to use. I'm especially drawn to high-stakes domains like healthcare and security, where getting it right matters more than getting it flashy.

Off the keyboard, you'll usually find me on a tennis court. It's the best debugging break I know, and a rally teaches the same thing a good model does: stay consistent, then adjust.
